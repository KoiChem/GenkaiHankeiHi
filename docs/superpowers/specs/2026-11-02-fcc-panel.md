# FCC operation-panel refinement and stereo strength

User-approved scope: beginner-friendly full-panel improvement, readable soft-blue visual hierarchy, clear unavailable actions, and a sibling strong-stereo toggle within the stereo frame. Existing model behavior and index.html are preserved.

## Decisions
- Use explicit opaque pale-blue panel surfaces, white outlined controls, and pale-blue selected controls with dark text and a second inner boundary. Selection uses aria-pressed rather than disabled selected choices. Avoid equally prominent solid-blue controls everywhere.
- Keep model recovery in the first utility row as 「位置を戻す」 (view-only action). Group view/stereo, crystal, observation, model-specific settings and display settings. Labels for grid counts are 1/4/8個; icons retain descriptive accessible names.
- Targets are at least 40px high (44px for most buttons), with separate sibling buttons and clear gaps. The stereo cycle and its 「強」 button share a bordered frame but neither is nested inside the other button.
- Strong stereo preserves mode, pose and preference; it multiplies the shared rendering/picking eye separation by 1.5. It is natively disabled outside stereo, with its enabling condition next to it. Perspective/orthographic choices remain hidden during stereo.
- Comparison is natively disabled while stereo is active, with a nearby explanation. Entering stereo from comparison still temporarily uses the void view; leaving stereo restores comparison.
- Restore actions are disabled only when no matching hidden objects exist. A visible reason explains that empty state. If opacity is zero, clarify that restoring flags alone does not make the object visible. Diagonal/snap controls and forced-opacity sliders likewise give short state-specific reasons next to the affected controls. Fullscreen is hidden when unavailable.
- Preserve focus while controls update; use a visible keyboard outline. Explicit text and control colors are contrast-tested rather than assuming pale colors are accessible. No claim of full WCAG/Digital Agency compliance is made.

## Primary references consulted
- [Digital Agency button usage](https://design.digital.go.jp/dads/components/button/usage/): action hierarchy and consistent grouping/placement.
- [Digital Agency button accessibility](https://design.digital.go.jp/dads/components/button/accessibility/): labels, focus order, target size, contrast and the need to explain unavoidable disabled controls nearby.
- [Digital Agency color accessibility](https://design.digital.go.jp/dads/foundations/color/accessibility/): explicit foreground/background colors, minimum text/control/focus contrast and non-color state cues.

These references inform a small static educational app; their entire design system is not being transplanted or claimed as certified compliance.
